Who Am I?

I'm Nipun — a first-year B.Tech student who made a simple but powerful decision: to stop being average. I'm studying at Rishihood University in collaboration with Newton School of Technology (NST), where I'm building a strong foundation in technology, one concept and one commit at a time.

I believe talent is overrated and consistency is underrated. I don't want to be the person who talks about doing big things — I want to be the one who quietly puts in the work until the results speak for themselves.

I'm a mix of two worlds: the tech world (Python, the command line, Git and vibe coding) and the energy world (cricket, badminton, the gym and a deep love for cars and driving). Together, they shape how I think — precise like code, fast like an engine, disciplined like an athlete.

My Story

CHAPTER 01 — THE AVERAGE PHASE

There was a time when I blended into the crowd. No clear goals, no fire, no direction. "I'll do it tomorrow" was my default setting, and the comfort zone felt like home. It was easy — and that was exactly the problem.

CHAPTER 02 — THE TURNING POINT

I realised that comfort doesn't build anything. Settling for "good enough" would only give me an average life. So I chose discomfort on purpose — I chose to learn, to build habits, and to hold myself to a higher standard.

CHAPTER 03 — THE RELENTLESS PRESENT

Today I'm a student at Rishihood University × NST, learning Python, the command line and Git, and exploring vibe coding. I wake up with purpose, train my body, play my sports, and code with intent. Every single day counts — and I treat it that way.

CHAPTER 04 — THE ROAD AHEAD

This is only the beginning. My goal is to become undeniable in tech — to build real-world projects, master my craft, and prove that average was only ever my starting line.

🧪

My Personal Theory

I call it the Breaking-Average Theory. Growth isn't magic — it's a formula:

Growth = ( Ambition × Discipline × Consistency ) ÷ Comfort

Ambition gives me direction, discipline gives me the engine, and consistency keeps it running every day. Comfort is the only thing I try to keep small — because the bigger it gets, the smaller my growth becomes.

🎯

What I'm Aiming For

To keep levelling up daily — master Python and the CLI, build projects that matter, grow into a skilled and confident engineer, and become someone who is respected for the work, not the words.

The Theory Behind My Passions

Technology — Building With Logic

Code is the closest thing to a superpower I've found. With Python, the command line and Git, an idea in my head can become something real on a screen. Vibe coding adds the creative flow — it reminds me that tech isn't just rules, it's imagination with structure. I'm not learning tech to pass exams; I'm learning it to build things that matter.

Cricket — Every Ball Is a New Chance

Cricket taught me that one mistake doesn't end the game. I love the T20 format because it's fast, aggressive and decisive — just like the mindset I want in life. Be an all-rounder: contribute with the bat, the ball and the brain. Whatever just happened, the next ball is a fresh opportunity.

💪

Gym & Badminton — Discipline in Motion

The gym doesn't care about your excuses, and neither does a badminton smash that needs perfect timing. Both reward the same thing: showing up again and again. Every rep and every rally builds the discipline that I then carry into my studies and my code. Consistency beats talent — every time.

🏎️

Cars & Driving — The Need for Speed

For me, cars are engineering, precision and freedom in one machine. The way an engine climbs through the gears is exactly how I want to grow — one shift at a time, always moving toward the redline. Speed isn't just on the road; it's a mindset of never idling.
